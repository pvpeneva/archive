Welcome

This tool lets you load, organize, filter, and summarize film archive sheets directly from Google Sheets using Apps Script.

How It Works

  • Connect to a Google Sheet via the Web App URL
  • Load and browse archive clips or still images
  • Filter, sort, and search through the database
  • Export or copy results
  • Get pricing + summary information
